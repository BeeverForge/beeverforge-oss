**A project's judging roles no longer follow another project's Bedrock or Vertex switch.** On a deployment where one project reaches its model through Bedrock and another through a different provider, the second project's product, tech-lead and sizer roles inherited `CLAUDE_CODE_USE_BEDROCK` from the worker's environment, chose Bedrock with no credentials and failed, and the product role answered that it could not see the requirements. The worktree box now withholds `CLAUDE_CODE_USE_BEDROCK` and `CLAUDE_CODE_USE_VERTEX` unless the project's `box.env` names them, as the container box already does.
