**The Pending tab's ↩ opens the conversation the item was asked in.** It only put the cursor in the box of the conversation already open, so an item asked in another of the person's conversations opened nothing they could see. It now opens that conversation (the project's room, the person's first conversation or one of their sessions) and puts the cursor in its box. An item recorded before this release, which does not say where it was asked, keeps the open box.
