**The people who may approve or act on a product are set and read by an action.** `product_people_set` writes the whole list of `{id, role}` to `product.admins` and `product.engineers` in one registry write, with `beeverforge product people set`; a person left out is removed, the rest of the entry is kept, a malformed entry, a person with two roles or a list past 200 is refused naming the entry, and a list equal to what is held answers `changed: false` without rewriting the file. It is the installation's: a scoped credential, or a person adding themselves, is denied. `product_people_list` reads it back with the same digest. The audit line carries the project, the counts and the digest, never an id.
