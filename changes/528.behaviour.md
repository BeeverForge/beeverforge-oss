**The people who may approve or act on a product are set and read by an action.** `product_people_set` writes the whole list of `{person, role}` to `product.admins` and `product.engineers` in one registry write, with `beeverforge product people set <name> person:role… [--file] [--yes]`, which shows the change and writes only with `--yes`; a person left out is removed, the rest of the entry is kept, a malformed entry, a person with two roles or a list past 1000, an id with a control character or past 256 characters is refused naming the entry, and a list equal to what is held answers `changed: false` without rewriting the file. It is the installation's: a scoped credential, anyone asking from a room, or a person adding themselves, is denied. `product_people_list` reads it back with the same digest. The audit line carries the project, the counts, the digest and the person ids added and removed, never a credential.
