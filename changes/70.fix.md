**`beeverforge box prove` now gives each gate the same time limit a job does**: its declared `timeout_minutes`, else the job's own default. A suite that needs longer than the default, and passes in every job, can now be proven instead of being stopped with exit 124 and holding pickup. A gate stopped at its limit now says so: the finding names the limit in seconds and the `timeout_minutes` to raise, instead of a bare `exited 124`.
