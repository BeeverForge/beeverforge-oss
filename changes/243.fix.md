**Enabling a project schedules its rounds at once, and disabling one stops them.** Enabling a project, from the panel, Slack or `beeverforge act`, now creates its tech-lead watch, knowledge refresh and card-question sweep schedules straight away, scoped to that project, instead of waiting for the next worker restart; disabling it deletes those three schedules, so its rounds stop firing. If the engine cannot be reached when the switch is turned, the switch still takes effect and the answer says the rounds were not changed. At boot the worker now also deletes the three schedules of any registered project that is disabled, as it already did for projects that are no longer registered.
