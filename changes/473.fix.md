**A project can be held to a number of jobs at once, so it no longer takes every slot of the floor.** The floor is shared by every project and the poller takes TO-DO cards in board order until it is full, so with a floor above 1 one project's cards could take every slot, and cards of one project that build on each other started together. With `BEEVERFORGE_JOBS_PER_PROJECT=N`, a project runs at most N jobs at once and its other cards wait in TO-DO in board order, while other projects' cards still start; unset or `0` keeps the floor alone, as before.
