**When the product role cannot answer, it says so, and the team hears why.** When the role's own model call failed (the harness refused the model, crashed, or was not logged in), the person was told "I can't see the product's requirements at the moment… I've flagged it to the team". The requirements had been read and nothing had been flagged: the cause was dropped, logged nowhere, and no impediment was filed. A failed draft read as "there was nothing to draft". The role now says it could not put an answer (or the draft) together, and promises an alert only when one is open. The cause is logged as `BEEVERFORGE_PRODUCT_CANNOT_ANSWER`, one impediment ("the product agent cannot answer: its model call fails") is filed per cause on the factory board and closed by the next answered turn, and the platform's own sentences are no longer handed back to the model as its earlier answers.
