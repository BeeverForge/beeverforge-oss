**The product index and the board are created readable by their owner only**, like the metrics store: the index (client conversation text), `board.db` (cards, comments and registered accounts), the board backup `project forget` takes, and their `-wal`/`-shm` files were created `0644`, readable by every user on the machine. Existing files are tightened to `0600` when they are opened. A symbolic link or a special file such as a FIFO at the store's path or at a `-wal`, `-shm` or `-journal` beside it is refused by name rather than followed or opened.
