**Reading a requirement takes time linear in its size.** The patterns that read a requirement's fields could span line breaks, so each blank line re-scanned every one after it: 50,000 blank lines took over a minute to read, and an empty field such as `- **Status:**` was read from the line below it. A title, field, heading or domain term followed by many spaces, and a value with many `<!--` in it, cost the same squared time within one line; they are now read in one pass.
