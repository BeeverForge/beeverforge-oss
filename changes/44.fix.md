**A stopped job now ends its agent and publishes nothing after the stop**: `stop` used to end only the job's workflow, and the agent pass went on in its worker thread, so a stopped job could still push, open a pull request, merge it and write on its card. Now the worker hears the stop at the job's next heartbeat: it kills the agent and every process the agent started, removes the job's worktree, and publishes nothing more (no push, pull request, review, merge, card move or ticket comment). `stop` now says what its worker was told: whether the worker was heard from recently and will act on the stop at its next heartbeat, or has gone silent so that nobody knows whether its agent is still running.
