**A tracker write that meets the forge's rate limit is applied after the reset, not lost.** A ticket state, label, assignee or comment written by a job, and the comments the worker posts on a card (the sizing and pre-flight verdicts, the gather's questions and chase, the preview, the coordinator's advice and the diagnosis), are queued when the forge answers with its rate limit and applied once the limit resets, each at most once, in order per ticket, with the newest state, label operation or assignee set per ticket winning. The queue is one file in the project's log directory, readable by its owner only, shared by the project's runners under a file lock, so it survives a restart; each entry is applied only on the repository it was made for. It is drained when a job starts and ends, on the next write, and on the worker's hourly round, so a project with no next job still applies it within the hour after the reset. A queued entry is dropped, logged, rather than move a card backwards: a state, label or assignee entry when the card's record holds a move recorded after it (a delivery, a park, a person's stop), a job's own progress marks when the job ends and hands back its outcome, and an assignee change when a person changed the assignees meanwhile. A stopped job applies nothing it queued, and the next drain judges those entries like any other. A comment is never superseded, and is applied whole. A job on a remote box keeps its queue on that machine, so what it had not applied by the job's end is lost with it. The card's own moves and comments are still converged by its hourly sweep, which does not yet wait for the reset.
