**A tracker write that meets the forge's rate limit is applied after the reset, not lost.** A ticket state, board move, label, assignee or comment written by a job, and the comments the worker posts on a card (the sizing and pre-flight verdicts, the gather's questions and chase, the preview, the coordinator's advice and the diagnosis), are queued when the forge answers with its rate limit and applied once the limit resets, each at most once, in order per ticket, with the newest state, label operation or assignee set per ticket winning. A GitHub project board's move refused by the rate limit is now raised as one and queued, where the state's label used to be written in its place and the move lost. Each entry is kept for its own card, so on a product of several repositories `acme/web#1` and `acme/api#1` are two tickets. The queue is one file in the project's log directory, readable by its owner only and shared by the project's runners, so it survives a restart: its lock is held to claim an entry and to record what its apply came to, never across the forge call, so two lanes write at the same time. A change the file could not take is kept in memory and written at the next operation, and is lost only if the process stops first. It is drained when a job starts and ends, on the next write, and on the worker's hourly round, so a project with no next job still applies it within the hour after the reset. A queued entry is dropped, logged, rather than move a card backwards: a state, label or assignee entry when the card's record holds a move recorded after it (a delivery, a park, a person's stop), a job's own progress marks when the job ends and hands back its outcome to the card's door, and an assignee change when a person changed the assignees meanwhile. A stopped job applies nothing it queued, and the next drain judges those entries like any other. A comment is never superseded, and is applied whole. A job on a remote box keeps its queue on that machine, so what it had not applied by the job's end is lost with it. The card's own moves and comments are still converged by its hourly sweep, which does not yet wait for the reset.
