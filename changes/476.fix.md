**A requirement's card whose title the redrafts left too long is shortened, not lost.** When a breakdown's card came back with a title over the 80-character limit and the redraft did not shorten it, the card was not filed — and when it was the requirement's only front, the whole breakdown ended with no card, though nothing else about it was wrong. Now, when the title's length is the only problem left, the title is shortened in whole words (its trailing clause first) and the card is checked once more by the floor and the judge; anything else they find still refuses it.
