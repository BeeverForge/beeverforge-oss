**The box proof pulls a private image the way the job does.** The proof built its box from its own copy of the job box's settings, and that copy had fallen behind: it never passed the image's pull credential (`box.pull_credential`). A proof of an image in a private registry pulled with no login, while the job's box pulled with one. The proof and the job now build their box from one function, so a setting the job's box gets reaches the proof with it.
