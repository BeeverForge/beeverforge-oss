**Under an Azure Boards `scope_epic`, the product role's cards and the factory's impediments must be put under the Epic by other means.** They are created in the project and linked to no parent, so they are outside the scope: the poller does not pick them up, the panel and the product role do not show them, and the search for an earlier filing does not find them, so a retry files another, and a scoped project refuses to edit, close, remove, queue or rank them until they are. On a factory board that is the project's own scoped tracker, an impediment is therefore never closed by the factory when the trouble clears, and the trouble's next occurrence, and its first after the worker restarts, files another beside it: close them in Azure Boards, or put each under the Epic, where the factory finds and closes it. A split's children are linked under the card they were split from, so they are in the scope whenever that card is, from the next read of the board; a child whose link failed, which the worker's log names (`could not link <child> under <parent>`), stays outside it, and a re-run of the split files another unless that child is put under its parent first. File the cards you want worked under a Feature of the Epic yourself. Starting a job on a card by its number, reopening a closed card and commenting on one still reach any work item of the project.
