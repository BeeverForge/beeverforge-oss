**The suite's coverage is measured, and CI fails below it.** `make coverage` runs the unit tests with coverage of `beeverforge/`, lines and branches, and reports it; on every push to main, CI measures the same in its four unit slices, and its `test` job fails when their combined total is under the floor in `pyproject.toml` (`[tool.coverage.report] fail_under`), naming the total measured and the floor. Pull requests are not measured, so a drop shows on main right after the merge that caused it; `make coverage` holds the floor before a merge. `docs/STATUS.md` publishes what CI measured (line 90.57%, branch 85.55%, total 89.38%, on `b5f2742`, the lower of two attempts) and the floor, 89.36%, which is that total less the spread between the attempts, and a pull request that raises coverage raises the floor and that figure together. On GitHub's runners the measurement costs each unit slice 1.1 to 2.1 times its time (median 1.9), which is why neither a pull request nor a plain `make test-unit` measures.
