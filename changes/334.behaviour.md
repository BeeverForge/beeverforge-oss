**The suite's coverage is measured, and CI fails below it.** `make coverage` runs the unit tests with coverage of `beeverforge/`, lines and branches, and reports it; on every push to main, CI measures the same in its four unit slices, and its `test` job fails when their combined total is under the floor in `pyproject.toml` (`[tool.coverage.report] fail_under`), naming the total measured and the floor. Pull requests are not measured, so a drop shows on main right after the merge that caused it; `make coverage` holds the floor before a merge. `docs/STATUS.md` publishes the baseline (line 90.57%, branch 85.53%, total 89.37%, measured on `b5f2742`), and a pull request that raises coverage raises the floor and that figure together. The measurement costs the unit tests 1.4 to 1.9 times their wall clock, which is why neither a pull request nor a plain `make test-unit` measures.
