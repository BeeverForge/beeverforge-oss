**A job parked on failing gates now says which gate failed and pushes the work it leaves behind.** The park said only "validations failed after N repair attempt(s)"; it now names each failing blocking gate (up to three, then how many more), its command and the last lines it printed, with any credential in them withheld — including a credential the gate's environment held under a name that does not look like one, such as `AZURE_DEVOPS_PAT` or a project's `token_env` — and with `#12` written `card 12`, and the journal keeps each gate's whole tail. The agent's commits are pushed to the job's branch, which the park names so a Resume continues them. When the agent wrote nothing, its changes could not be committed, or the push fails, the park says nothing was pushed, why, and where the work is: on the worktree box, the local branch and its repository, to push by hand before resuming; on the container box, that it was discarded. **A push no longer replaces commits it did not start from without keeping them.** Every push of a job's branch is `--force-with-lease` over the tip the job started from (or over nothing, for a fresh start). When the branch on the forge holds commits the job did not make — a person's fix on a parked branch, the work a park preserved there, a re-run's leftover — they are first copied to a branch of their own, `<branch>-kept-<commit>`, the card and the journal name it, and the job's push then lands over them, so a self-heal resume or a re-run of the card still finishes unattended and nothing is lost. Only when that copy cannot be made, or the branch moves again before the push lands, is the job held, naming the branch.
