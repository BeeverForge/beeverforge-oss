**A card relabelled out of a project's Jira slice can no longer be closed, removed or corrected from that project.** The product role kept such a card in its board snapshot for up to six hours, until the next full sweep, and closed, removed or rewrote it on request; it now asks the slice first, as queueing already did, and answers that the card is another project's, or that it could not reach the board when the slice cannot be asked.
