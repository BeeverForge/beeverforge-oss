**An expired box proof is now proven again by the worker, so pickup resumes without a person running `box prove`.** The worker starts one `box_prove` for the expiry — once per hold reason, only when the floor has a free job slot, which the proof holds while it runs — and the next tick after it passes clears the hold and tells the channel once that the box was proven again and why. When it fails, pickup stays held, the hold's sentence becomes the proof's own failure with its remedy, and the worker does not try that reason again; a box never proven is not proven by the worker. Set `BEEVERFORGE_BOX_REPROVE=off` to keep proving by hand.
