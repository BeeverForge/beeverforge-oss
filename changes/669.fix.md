**A card the sizer splits is no longer counted as a merge.** The job ended `done`, one of the states past the merge, so throughput (`past_the_merge`, the cost per merged ticket) and autonomy counted a card that shipped nothing, and counted each of its parts again when they merged. It now ends `split`, a state of its own, and each part counts once, when it merges. The board is unchanged: the parent closes as not delivered and the parts are filed as before.
