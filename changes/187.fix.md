**Approving a staged proposal whose write did not happen no longer answers success.** When the product role refused the write, `product_answer` now answers that refusal's code (403, 404, 409 or 400) with `data.refusal`, and when the write broke — a staged breakdown whose engine did not answer included — it answers 500 `failed` with a `data.reference`; `beeverforge product ask … --propose --yes` exits non-zero for both, and the panel no longer draws them as an ordinary answer. A write that waited for the product's other writes and did not get its turn is still staged again and answered `ok: true`, with the sentence telling the person nothing was recorded and to ask again (#269).
